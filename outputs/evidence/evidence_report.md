# Evidence from past calls

Generated by `src/evidence_analysis.py` from Apr-Sep 2026 bot calls, turn transcripts and evaluator outputs.
Strength: **strong** = n>=300 per group and non-overlapping 95% CIs with >=3pp gap; **moderate** = n>=100 and |z|>=1.96; **weak** = direction only.

## E1_personalisation
**Claim:** A personalised script (seller-specific opening) fixes more meetings than the standard VANI script.  
**Metric:** Meeting Fixed rate, answered calls, restricted to the lead buckets the trial ran on  
- personalised-script trial: 14.2% (95% CI 11.2%-17.7%, n=452)
- main_vani: 11.5% (95% CI 11.3%-11.6%, n=203,046)
- Difference: +2.7pp, z=1.81
- confound: `"Trial is 452 calls; campaigns label General/Not-Interested differently, so NI is NOT compared across versions."`
**Strength:** weak  
**Verdict on the claim:** inconclusive  
**Used by:** conversation_plan.personalisation, conversation_plan.opening

## E2_interrupted_opening
**Claim:** When a seller cuts VANI's opening pitch off mid-sentence, the call is far more likely to be lost -- interruption is an early rush/impatience signal.  
**Metric:** Not Interested rate  
- seller interrupted the opening: 42.5% (95% CI 39.1%-45.9%, n=810)
- opening delivered in full: 28.0% (95% CI 27.2%-28.9%, n=11,022)
- Difference: +14.4pp, z=8.73
**Strength:** strong  
**Verdict on the claim:** supported  
**Used by:** live_adaptation.rush, voice.pace, conversation_plan.opening

## E2b_brevity_for_terse_sellers
**Claim:** Among fully-delivered openings, terse sellers (1-3 word replies) respond better to a short opening (<=30 words).  
**Metric:** Meeting Fixed rate, terse sellers, non-interrupted openings only  
- opening <=30 words: 16.7% (95% CI 15.3%-18.2%, n=2,496)
- opening >30 words: 35.4% (95% CI 33.1%-37.8%, n=1,641)
- Difference: -18.7pp, z=-13.75
- confound: `"Opening length still varies with campaign script, not randomised."`
**Strength:** strong  
**Verdict on the claim:** contradicted  
**Used by:** voice.pace, conversation_plan.opening

## E3_pace
**Claim:** Delivery speed matters differently by seller type: terse sellers tolerate faster delivery; chatty sellers do better slower.  
**Metric:** Meeting Fixed rate; fast = top third of bot words/sec, slow = bottom third  
- bot_wps_terciles: `[2.95, 3.35]`
- terse_sellers_fast_vs_slow: A 18.1% (n=1457) vs B 29.2% (n=1594), -11.1pp, strong, contradicted
- chatty_sellers_fast_vs_slow: A 12.2% (n=1314) vs B 28.6% (n=1149), -16.5pp, strong, supported
- confound: `"Bot speech rate is not randomised; it partly reflects how much the bot said, not only TTS pace."`
**Strength:** strong  
**Used by:** voice.pace

## E5_repetition
**Claim:** In calls long enough to repeat, a VANI that repeats itself (same question/point again) fixes fewer meetings.  
**Metric:** Meeting Fixed rate, calls with >=10 turns only (controls for call length)  
- bot repeated itself: 51.4% (95% CI 48.7%-54.1%, n=1,328)
- no bot repetition: 33.4% (95% CI 32.0%-34.7%, n=4,856)
- Difference: +18.1pp, z=12.07
- uncontrolled_ni_comparison_for_transparency: A 18.7% (n=1736) vs B 32.7% (n=11412), -14.1pp, strong
- confound: `"Uncontrolled, repetition looks protective only because short calls cannot repeat."`
**Strength:** strong  
**Verdict on the claim:** contradicted  
**Used by:** conversation_plan.guardrails.ask_limits, live_adaptation.deferral

**Used by:** engineering: regex fast-path before LLM fallback, voice.pace
